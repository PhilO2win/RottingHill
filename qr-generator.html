QR Code Generator

Generate printable QR codes for all 6 buildings of Rotting Hill Borough.

How to use: Enter the URL where your village is hosted (e.g. https://rotting-hill.netlify.app), click Generate, then print this page. Cut out each card and mount it inside the corresponding building's front door — behind a hinged door panel so players must physically open the door to scan. Players scan with any phone camera — no app needed.

Free hosting: Drag all HTML files to netlify.com/drop for a free instant URL. Then return here and update the base URL.
